# Brand colors

Four chromatic spokes around navy (`#0a1628`) as the structural primary:

| Token | Hex | Wheel role | Use |
|-------|-----|------------|-----|
| **Green** (`secondary`) | `#2d5a47` | Warm-cool trust | Secondary actions, success, NPRM emphasis |
| **Gold** (`accent`) | `#d4af37` | Brand highlight | CTAs, active nav, chart hover — not status chips |
| **Blue** (`blue` / `info`) | `#2f6f8f` | Opposite of gold | Data/analysis chrome, info callouts, cool support |
| **Rose** (`rose`) | `#9a5b6e` | Opposite of green | Sparse status only: New, Soon, Beta, Planned |

Copper (`#b87333`) remains a warm warning / heritage utility, not a fifth brand spoke.

Use rose sparingly. Prefer blue when something is informational rather than “new/soon.”
